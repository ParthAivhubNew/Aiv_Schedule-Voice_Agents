# Outreach glossary

One name for each thing, used in menus, page titles, buttons, search and help (plan 13, step 8). When a screen needs a new
word, check here first; when a name changes, change it here and everywhere it shows.

## The product
| Say | Not | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach | the client's name, "the platform" | The product. The company's own name shows only in the sidebar's account badge. |
| app | plugin, module | Leads, Voice and Social. "Add Voice", "the Voice app". |
| Leads | Lead Gen, Lead Generation | The app for finding companies and emailing them. |
| Voice | Calling, Voice AI | The app for AI phone calls. |
| Social | Scheduler, Social Media Scheduler | The app for planning and publishing posts. |

## Setting things up
| Say | Not | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Settings | Admin, Setup, Configuration | One entry per app menu, and the company-wide Settings page |

| Brand look | Theme | Social: logo, colours, how posts sound |

## Words on buttons
- **Open** opens a thing; **See plans** goes to plans; **Top up** buys credits; **Try it first** opens an app without a plan.
- **Import** brings a spreadsheet in; **Export** takes one out.
- **Clear all** empties filters; **Start again** / **Clear and start again** also empties results.
- Staff-only screens (the staff portal, AI configuration) may name suppliers; client screens never do (brand.test.js).
