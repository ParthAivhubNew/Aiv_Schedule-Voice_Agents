OUTREACH FOR {{COMPANY}}
========================

Everything you need is in this folder. Your key is already filled in. There is nothing to add to your server.

STEP 1 - Try it (2 minutes)
  Open a terminal in this folder and start any small web server, for example:
        python -m http.server 8000
  Then open  http://localhost:8000/outreach-test.html  in your browser.
  Click "Connect Outreach", sign in or create an account in the small window, and the screen opens.
  (Open it through the server, not by double-clicking the file: a page opened from disk has no address Outreach can check.)

STEP 2 - Put it in your own page
  Copy the two lines in  snippet.html  to the place in your page where Outreach should show.
  Change the colours on the same line: data-accent, data-radius, data-font, data-bg, data-text, data-logo, data-name.
  Other screens: change  data-outreach="leadgen"  (the whole Leads app) to  voice  or  social  (also: leads, campaigns, mail for a smaller slice).
  Where the sign-in shows: add  data-login="popup"  (small window, the default),  "inline"  (inside your page) or  "page"  (the whole window, then back).
  Show it in a popup or side panel instead: use a button, like  <button data-outreach="voice" data-mode="popup">Calls</button>

STEP 3 - Add your live address
  In Outreach: Partner programme > Web addresses. Add the address your page runs on, like  https://app.yourproduct.com
  While you test, pages on your own computer (localhost) work without adding anything.
  When you are ready, press "Go live" in the same place.

USE YOUR OWN DATA  (from your own code, after the person has connected)
  Outreach.import([{ name: "Acme Ltd", email: "a@acme.test" }], { list: "My customers" })   // their account gets your records
  Outreach.run("voice", "call.list")                                                          // any action of any plugin
  Outreach.find("Acme Ltd")                                                                   // open Leads with that company searched
  While you are in test mode these are simulated and save nothing. See the guide "Get started".

OPTIONAL - The Outreach browser extension
  Chrome or Edge > chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) > turn on Developer mode > Load unpacked > choose the  extension  folder.
  Firefox > about:debugging > This Firefox > Load Temporary Add-on > choose  extension-firefox/manifest.json.
  Click the Outreach button in the toolbar. Your team gets Outreach in a side panel on any page.
  Click it on a page you are reading and the panel offers to look up that company in Leads. It reads the page only when you click,
  and only that page. (Safari: not available yet.)

Your publishable key  {{CLIENT_ID}}  is safe to put in a page. There is no secret key in this kit.
